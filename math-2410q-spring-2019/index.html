Exams.

Late Policy: There will (generally) be no make-up exams granted. If you have to miss an exam for a legitimate reason, other exams may be weighted differently. Other options may be discussed if necessary. Challenging a Grade on Exams: From the time your exam is returned, you have 48 hours to challenge your grade.
