Practice

It is strongly encouraged that you try the problems without hints or solutions first.

Traditionally, it can be normal to spend more than 30 minutes on a given problem when first learning the material.

Taking this time is a good thing.

Warning: These problems were added to provide practice. They have absolutely no influence on exam design. This is an experiment.

Some of these problems draw on Jiří Lebl’s Basic Analysis, OpenStax’s Calculus Volume 1, and earlier 18.100A problem sets.
